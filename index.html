Why Choose Burnham?

A unique combination of heritage, sport and community.

🏆 Historic Heritage

Founded in 1899, Burnham is one of the UK's longest established target shooting clubs.

🎯 Expert Guidance

Learn safely from experienced NSRA Certified Range Officers, Club Instructors and ISSF Certified Coaches.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Friendly

A welcoming environment for adults, families and junior shooters.

🧩 NRA Affiliated Club

This provides access to MOD and Bisley ranges, competition entry, training courses, and public and employers' liability insurance.

🧩 NSRA Affiliated Club

NSRA affiliation provides governing body support, club insurance, coaching qualifications, and access to competitions.

🧩 BCRA Member Club

Buckinghamshire Rifle Association promotes Target Shooting in the County - including Smallbore, Fullbore , Gallery Rifle, Air Pistol and Rifle

JUNIOR DEVELOPMENT PROGRAMME

Building Confidence Through Sport

Target shooting develops concentration, discipline, responsibility and self-confidence.

  • Foundation & Safety
  • Technical Development
  • Performance Building
  • Mental Resilience
  • Competition Preparation
  • NSRA Competition
  • British Shooting Nominated Meetings
Try Shooting > Learn Safety > Club Practice > Competition > Long-Term Development

This programme is designed to produce athletes capable of shooting a complete ISSF qualification and final, and establish a clear pathway from junior beginner through to club, regional and ultimately national-level competition while maintaining enjoyment, safety and long-term athlete development.
